Who Is Galadriel?

Galadriel was the Ñoldorin Lady of the woods of Lothlórien who witnessed Middle-earth across three Ages. She is described as "the mightiest and fairest of all the Elves that remained in Middle-earth" and the "greatest of Elven women." During the War of the Ring, she revealed to Frodo Baggins that she was the bearer of Nenya, one of the Three Elven Rings.

"In place of a Dark Lord, you would have a queen, not dark but beautiful and terrible as the dawn!"

Life in Aman

Galadriel was born in Eldamar in the First Age, the fourth child of Finarfin, prince of the Ñoldor. She grew beautiful and tall, even for the women of the Ñoldor, being strong of body, will, and mind — "a match for both the loremasters and the athletes of the Eldar in the days of their youth."

Her hair was held to be an unmatched marvel, mingling and surpassing the gold of her father and the starlike silver of her mother. The Eldar said that both lights of the Two Trees of Valinor were captured in her tresses. It was said that this first gave Fëanor the idea of capturing the merged light of the Trees, which would result in the making of the Silmarils. He once asked her three times for one of her tresses, but she always refused.

In Middle-earth

Galadriel took part in the Exile of the Ñoldor but opposed the Kinslaying at Alqualondë. After arriving in Beleriand, she lived mainly in Doriath, where she befriended Melian. With her husband Celeborn, she later ruled over the woodland Elves of Lothlórien, and together they had a daughter, Celebrían, who married Elrond.

Powers & Abilities

As one of the oldest Elves in Middle-earth, Galadriel possessed great magic and spiritual powers. She could use a telepathy-like ability (Ósanwë) to read minds and hearts or communicate mentally over vast distances. She had a degree of precognition, glimpsing possible futures through her Mirror, and could confer the Light of Valinor — a purely benign light capable of purifying evil.

As the bearer of Nenya (Ring of Water), she had powers of concealment and preservation. With it, she enveloped her entire realm in a magical mist, keeping it frozen in time and protected from corruption and enemy attacks.

Appearance

Galadriel is portrayed as an Elf of extraordinary beauty: tall, pale, with long, lustrous golden-blonde hair with silver highlights and grey eyes as piercing as starlight spears. She wears long white and gold tunics and gowns, and headdresses that symbolise her status as ruler.
