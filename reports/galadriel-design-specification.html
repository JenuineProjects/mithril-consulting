Galadriel's Design Specification — Iteration 11

Mithril Consulting | Client: Ticketmaster
Agent: Galadriel (Lead Designer)
Slogan: "See what could be."
Date: 14 March 2026
Note: Builds on Iteration 10 design — retains all core features, adds social layer, post-event engagement, Fair Price tooltip, confirmation screen, and enhanced search.

1. HANDOFF ACKNOWLEDGMENT

I have received Saruman's enhanced Research Brief, approved by Aragorn with the instruction to retain all Iteration 10 features and add: (1) a social layer, (2) post-event engagement with "Your Year in Live," (3) social privacy controls, and (4) the Fair Price Indicator tappable tooltip.

Three new findings shaped my design additions:

  1. No ticketing platform has a "Year in Live" feature — this is a first-mover opportunity with Spotify Wrapped as the proven engagement model (200M users, 500M shares, 64% retention improvement).
  2. Live events are inherently social but the app is entirely solitary — every competitor has some social features; Ticketmaster has none.
  3. Privacy controls must be as prominent as social features — Saruman's GDPR guidance requires granular opt-in, not default-on social sharing.

2. DESIGN VISION

Retained from Iteration 10: "When a customer opens the Ticketmaster app, they should feel the same trust they feel when a knowledgeable friend recommends a show."

Extended for Iteration 11: "...and after the show, they should feel like part of a community that celebrates live music together."

The app transforms from a transactional search tool (Iteration 1–9) through a trusted companion (Iteration 10) into a social platform for live entertainment (Iteration 11) — one that connects fans before, during, and after events.

3. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

  1. Transparency over surprise — Retained. All-in pricing from first display. (Saruman: #1 complaint, FTC imperative)
  2. Calm over urgency — Retained. Reduce anxiety at every step. (Saruman: queue crashes, dynamic pricing)
  3. Evidence over opinion — Retained. Every decision traces to research. (Saruman: customers distrust claims)
  4. Human backup always available — Retained. ARIA always offers human escalation. (GDPR Article 22)
  5. Personalisation with permission — Retained. AI transparency, user control. (EU AI Act Article 50)
  6. Social by invitation, not by default — NEW. Social features are opt-in. Friend activity, attendance sharing, and group features require explicit consent. Privacy controls are as easy to find as social features. (Saruman: GDPR granular consent, data minimisation)

4. SOLUTION CONCEPTS

Solution Concept 1: Transparent All-In Pricing (RETAINED + ENHANCED)

Unchanged from Iteration 10. All prices include all fees. Price Breakdown modal available everywhere.

Iteration 11 enhancement — Fair Price Indicator Tooltip:

When a user taps the Fair Price Indicator badge ("Great Value" / "Fair Price" / "Premium"), a tooltip overlay appears:

  • "Great Value" → "This is below average for similar events. Similar seated events at this venue average €65–€85."
  • "Fair Price" → "This is a typical price for this type of event. Similar events range €80–€120."
  • "Premium" → "This is above average — high demand and premium seating. Standard tickets for this event start at €X."

The tooltip closes on tap-away or a "Got it" button. It is not a modal — it is a lightweight overlay anchored to the badge.

Solution Concept 2: Personalised Discovery with Social Layer (ENHANCED)

Retained from Iteration 10 + new social features.

New social features:

  • Friend Activity Feed: A section on the Home screen: "Friends going to events." Shows which friends (from connected contacts, with consent) are attending or interested in upcoming events. Format: "[Friend name] is going to [Event]" with event card.
  • Group Booking: From any event detail, a "Go Together" button. Opens a shareable link. Friends tap the link, select their tickets, and pay individually — but the group is linked for coordination. Group chat via ARIA for logistics.
  • Social Discovery: "Popular with your friends" recommendation section powered by social filtering — events that multiple friends are interested in surface higher.
  • Post-Purchase Sharing: After buying tickets, a prompt: "Invite friends to [Event]?" with a share link to messaging apps.

Privacy controls:

  • Social features require opt-in during onboarding (new onboarding screen) or in Settings → Privacy.
  • Toggles: "Share my attendance with friends" (ON/OFF), "Show me friends' events" (ON/OFF), "Allow group invitations" (ON/OFF).
  • Default state: OFF. Social features are invitation-based, not default-on.
  • AI disclosure: "Friend recommendations powered by AI based on your connections and attendance. [Manage privacy]"

Solution Concept 3: ARIA — AI Assistant (RETAINED + ENHANCED)

Retained from Iteration 10: Support Mode + Queue Companion Mode + human escalation.

Iteration 11 enhancements:

  • Expanded query types: Refund assistance ("I need a refund for [Event]" → ARIA checks refund policy and walks through process), event rescheduling help, accessibility queries ("Is [Venue] wheelchair accessible?"), and proactive context-based suggestions.
  • Group coordination mode: During group bookings, ARIA assists with logistics: "Your group for Oasis has 4 of 6 tickets confirmed. Want me to send a reminder to the remaining friends?"
  • Post-event mode: After an event, ARIA proactively offers: "How was Hozier last night? Rate your experience, or see similar events coming up."

Solution Concept 4: Post-Event Engagement — "Your Year in Live" (NEW)

Problem (from Saruman): Zero post-event engagement. The relationship ends at the venue door. No loyalty loop.

Solution — three tiers of post-event engagement:

Tier 1 — Post-Event Recap (within 24 hours of event):

A card appears in My Tickets → Past Events:

  • Event name, date, venue
  • "Rate your experience" (1–5 stars + optional comment)
  • "Events like this coming up" — 2–3 AI recommendations based on the attended event
  • "Share your experience" — shareable card for social media with event details

Tier 2 — Monthly Summary:

A notification/card: "Your month in live." Shows events attended that month, genres explored, and upcoming recommendations. Lightweight — not intrusive.

Tier 3 — "Your Year in Live" (Annual Retrospective):

A full-screen, swipeable experience (inspired by Spotify Wrapped) that shows:

  • Total events attended
  • Top genres and artists
  • Favourite venue (most visited)
  • Total hours of live entertainment
  • "Concert companion" — friend you attended the most events with (if social features are enabled)
  • Shareable card optimised for Instagram Stories / TikTok (vertical format, branded, visually striking)

Why this works: Spotify Wrapped 2025 reached 200M+ users in 24 hours and was shared 500M+ times. It drives both engagement and organic marketing. Ticketmaster has the richest dataset in live entertainment — who went where, when. "Your Year in Live" transforms that data from internal analytics into customer-facing engagement.

AI enhancement: Recommender System generates the recap and recommendations. AI disclosure: "Your recap is generated from your attendance history. [Manage your data]"

New Screens

Screen 7: Confirmation / Success (NEW — addressing Iteration 10 gap)

Layout: Full celebration screen. Checkmark icon with confetti-style accent. Event name, date, venue, section, and all-in price confirmed. QR code preview. Three action buttons: "My Tickets" (view ticket), "Add to Calendar," "Invite Friends" (share link).

Interaction:

  • Tap "My Tickets" → navigates to My Tickets screen
  • Tap "Add to Calendar" → shows confirmation toast
  • Tap "Invite Friends" → opens share sheet with pre-written message: "I'm going to [Event]! Join me — [link]"

States:

  • Default: Confirmation with all ticket details
  • Group booking: Shows group status: "3 of 5 friends have booked. [Send reminder]"

Accessibility: All confirmation details readable by screen reader. QR code has text alternative.

Screen 8: Group Booking Flow (NEW)

Layout: Initiated from Event Detail → "Go Together." Shows: event name, a shareable link, list of invited friends with booking status (Invited / Booked / Declined), and a group chat link via ARIA.

Interaction:

  • Tap "Share Link" → copies link or opens share sheet
  • Tap "Chat with Group" → opens ARIA in group coordination mode
  • See friend status update in real time (simulated in prototype)

States:

  • Default: Link generated, no friends yet
  • In progress: 2 of 4 friends booked, others pending
  • Complete: All friends booked — "Your group is set! See you at [Event]."

Accessibility: Status updates announced to screen readers. All interactive elements meet 44×44px minimum.

Screen 9: "Your Year in Live" Retrospective (NEW)

Layout: Full-screen, swipeable cards (5 cards in sequence):

  1. "Your Year in Live 2026" — title card with user name and year
  2. "[X] events attended" — number with genre breakdown visual
  3. "Your top artist: [Name]" — with total times seen
  4. "Your favourite venue: [Name]" — with total visits
  5. "Share your year" — shareable card with summary stats + "Share to Stories" button

Interaction:

  • Swipe left/right between cards
  • Tap "Share to Stories" → generates a branded vertical image optimised for Instagram Stories / TikTok
  • Tap "Done" → returns to My Tickets

States:

  • Default: Full retrospective loaded
  • Insufficient data: "You attended [X] events this year. Come back next year for your full Year in Live!" (if fewer than 3 events)

AI disclosure: "Your Year in Live is generated from your attendance history. [Manage your data]"

Accessibility: Swipeable cards have alternative navigation (next/previous buttons). All statistics available as text.

Screen 10: Onboarding (ENHANCED — 4 screens, was 3)

  • Screen 10a — Welcome: Retained from Iteration 10
  • Screen 10b — Personalisation Setup: Retained (genre selection)
  • Screen 10c — Social Features: NEW. "Connect with friends. See events friends are going to, coordinate group bookings, and share your experiences. All social features are opt-in — you control what's shared."

Toggles: Share my attendance / Show friends' events / Allow group invitations. Default: OFF.

AI disclosure: "Social features use AI to match events with friend activity. [Learn more]"

6. AI FEATURE SPECIFICATIONS

Retained from Iteration 10

  1. Personalised Recommendation Engine (Recommender System) — unchanged
  2. Fair Price Indicator (Intelligent Agent) — enhanced with tappable tooltip
  3. ARIA (Chatbot/Intelligent Agent hybrid) — enhanced with expanded query types and group coordination

New AI Feature

4. Social Discovery & "Your Year in Live" Engine

What it does for the user: Surfaces events friends are attending, generates "Popular with your friends" recommendations, creates post-event recaps, and produces the annual "Your Year in Live" retrospective.

AI system type: Recommender System — uses social filtering (friend connections + attendance data) alongside the existing collaborative and content-based filtering.

Data needed: User attendance history, friend connections (opted-in only), friend attendance (opted-in only), event metadata (genre, artist, venue).

What it shows the user: Friend activity cards, social recommendations with explanations ("Popular with your friends"), post-event recaps, and annual retrospective. All with AI disclosure.

When it fails or is unavailable: Social sections hidden if user hasn't opted in or has no connected friends. Retrospective shows minimum viable version if insufficient data.

Privacy: All social data processing requires explicit opt-in. Friend data is never used without both parties' consent. Users can delete all social data at any time.

7. ACCESSIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

Retained from Iteration 10 (all requirements still apply) plus:

  • Swipeable "Year in Live" cards have next/previous button alternatives for users who cannot swipe
  • Social activity updates use ARIA live regions so screen readers announce friend booking confirmations
  • Group booking status is available as structured text, not just visual indicators
  • Social toggle controls in onboarding and Settings are clearly labelled and keyboard-accessible

8. NOTES FOR GIMLI

Non-Negotiable (do not change):

  • All Iteration 10 non-negotiables remain: all-in pricing, AI disclosures, ARIA human escalation, queue price guarantee, phone frame spec
  • Fair Price Indicator tooltip — must be tappable with contextual explanation text
  • Confirmation screen — must exist as a proper screen (not a toast/alert)
  • Social features default to OFF — opt-in only, never default-on
  • AI disclosure on all social/friend features — users must know when AI is using social data

Flexible Areas:

  • "Your Year in Live" depth — can be simplified to 3 cards instead of 5 if build time is constrained. The shareable card is the priority.
  • Group booking real-time updates — can be simulated with pre-set states rather than live WebSocket updates
  • Monthly summary — can be deferred if post-event recap + annual retrospective are built. The immediate post-event recap is the priority.
  • ARIA group coordination — can be simplified to pre-scripted responses for group scenarios
  • Social proof ("Sarah and 12 others are going") — can use pre-set data rather than live friend matching

Presentation Notes (from client feedback):

  • Use real images (Unsplash) for event cards — no emoji placeholders
  • Include an info panel beside the phone frame with journey guides and feature descriptions (see example prototype for reference)
  • Make customer journeys clearly accessible — the demo must have clear entry points for each journey
