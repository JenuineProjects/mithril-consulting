2. MESSAGING FRAMEWORK

Core Message

"The price you see is the price you pay. The friends you go with make it better. And the memories stay with you all year."

This extends the Iteration 10 message (trust through pricing) with the two new pillars: social connection and lasting engagement. It tells customers the app isn't just honest — it's built for how people actually experience live events: together, and with memories worth keeping.

Key Proof Points

  1. All-in pricing with Fair Price context. Every price includes all fees. Tap the Fair Price badge to see why it's rated "Great Value" or "Premium" — with a plain-English explanation comparing to similar events. Not just transparent — contextual. (BUILT TO SPEC)
  2. "Your Year in Live." At the end of the year, see your story: events attended, top artist, favourite venue, shareable card. Your live music year, celebrated. (BUILT TO SPEC — shareable card visual is built; actual Stories export requires production implementation)
  3. Go together. See which friends are going to events. Invite friends from the confirmation screen. Coordinate group bookings. Live events are social — the app should be too. (BUILT TO SPEC — friend data is simulated in prototype; production requires contacts integration)
  4. ARIA + human agents. Instant help for orders, refunds, transfers, accessibility info. One tap to a real human who has your full conversation. (BUILT TO SPEC)
  5. Queue price guarantee. Price locked when you join. No dynamic pricing while you wait. (BUILT TO SPEC)

Tone of Voice

Retained from Iteration 10: Direct, warm, honest. Sound like a friend, not a corporation. Specific over vague.

New for Iteration 11: The social and post-event features allow a warmer, more celebratory tone. Ticketmaster can now talk about moments and memories, not just transactions. But only where the features back it up.

What NOT to Say

  • Do not promise live Stories/TikTok sharing — the shareable card is visual-only in the prototype
  • Do not claim real-time group coordination — group booking uses pre-set states
  • Do not imply a live social network — friend data requires contacts integration
  • Do not promise monthly summaries — deferred
  • All Iteration 10 restrictions still apply (no claiming features as "completely rebuilt," etc.)

3. TRUST-REBUILDING NARRATIVE

What Ticketmaster Is Acknowledging

Retained from Iteration 10: acknowledgment of hidden fees, queue crashes, and chatbot failures.

Extended for Iteration 11:

We also know that buying tickets has been a lonely experience. You go to a concert with friends — but you buy tickets alone. You have an incredible night — and the app has nothing to say about it the next morning. No "how was the show?" No "your friend is going to something similar." No memory of the experience at all.

We built the app for transactions. We forgot it should be for experiences.

What Specifically Has Changed

Retained from Iteration 10 (3 changes):

  1. Every price includes all fees. Tap the badge to see why it's "Great Value" or "Premium."
  2. ARIA helps instantly. One tap to a real human.
  3. Queue prices are locked. No dynamic pricing while you wait.

New for Iteration 11 (3 additional changes):

  1. See who's going. If your friends use Ticketmaster and you both opt in, you'll see events they're attending. Tap "Go Together" to coordinate. No one books alone unless they want to.
  2. After the show, we're still here. The morning after a concert, you'll see a recap in your tickets: what you saw, events like it coming up, a chance to rate and share. The experience doesn't end at the door.
  3. Your Year in Live. At the end of the year, see your live music story: events attended, top artist, favourite venue. Share it. Celebrate it. It's yours.

How Customers Can Verify It

  • Pricing: Open any event. The price is the total. Tap the badge — see the context. Tap "Price Breakdown" — see the maths.
  • Support: Open ARIA. Ask about a refund. Then tap "Talk to a Human." See the handoff.
  • Social: Turn on social features in Settings. See friends' activity. Tap "Go Together." It works.
  • Post-event: Check My Tickets → Past. See your recaps. Tap "Your Year in Live."

We're not asking you to trust us. We're asking you to check.
